# ORBIT landing brief

Product: a fictional workspace for small teams to organize, run and review AI workflows.
Audience: developers, tech leads and small product teams.
Goal: make the visitor explore the interactive 3D demo and understand the product concept.
Language: English for all user-facing page copy.
Scope: a presentation-only landing; no functional AI backend or account system.

Hero:
- Eyebrow: AI WORKFLOW SPACE
- Headline: Your AI workflows. In one orbit.
- Copy: Bring context, tools and checks together in one focused workspace for your team.
- CTA: Explore the core
- CTA destination: the on-page interactive 3D stage, identified by `#demo`.

Visual:
- Dark graphite background.
- Cool teal as the primary accent, with restrained violet highlights.
- One central 3D object: AI Core, with a compact center and three open orbital rings.
- Give the rings slightly different angles to create a recognizable silhouette.
- Precise, technical, minimal; no stock imagery.
- Desktop hero: text on the left, 3D object on the right.
- At 390 px mobile width: text and CTA first, then the 3D object.
- Begin with soft lighting and subtle motion; add glow only if it improves readability.

Sections: hero, three capabilities, workflow, final CTA.

Capabilities:
- Shared context: keep task instructions and project knowledge together.
- Connected tools: bring the tools needed for a workflow into one workspace.
- Review checkpoints: check results before treating a task as complete.

Workflow:
1. Define: describe the task and provide its context.
2. Connect: choose the tools needed for the workflow.
3. Review: inspect the result and decide what happens next.

Demo:
- The interactive AI Core is the landing's demo; do not imply that a working AI service exists.
- Start with a subtle pointer response and gentle idle rotation.
- During a later customization step, add an accessible control to pause and resume idle rotation.
- Disable idle rotation when reduced motion is requested.
- Provide a static fallback when 3D is unavailable.

Final CTA:
- Headline: Give your next workflow a center.
- CTA: Explore the core
- Destination: `#demo`.
